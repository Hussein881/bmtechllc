# Engagement Kickoff Runbook

The first week sets the tone for everything that follows. This runbook exists so
kickoff is a repeatable sequence rather than an improvisation.

> [!NOTE]
> This page is a draft. The sequence below reflects how the last two engagements
> actually ran, but it has not yet been used to run one prospectively.

## Day 0 — Contract countersigned

| Step | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| File the countersigned SOW | ah | Store alongside the engagement folder |
| Create `content/engagements/{client}/` | ah | Working notes live here from day one |
| Draft the kickoff agenda | ah | Circulate at least 48h before the session |

## Day 1–2 — Access and logistics

Chase access early. Waiting on credentials is the single most common cause of a
wasted first week.

- Request the accounts and data access discovery will depend on
- Confirm the three stakeholder interview slots are booked
- Verify we can reach any system we are expected to integrate with

## Day 3 — Kickoff session

Run 90 minutes with the executive sponsor, operational owner, and technical lead
in the same room.

The agenda is fixed:

1. **Restate the problem** in the client's own words, then ask what we got wrong.
2. **Walk the delivery plan** — milestones, review points, what "done" means.
3. **Name the risks** we already see, and ask what we are missing.
4. **Confirm the communication rhythm** — async written updates, escalation path.
5. **Agree the decision log** — where decisions get recorded and who signs off.

## Day 4–5 — First working session

Begin discovery proper. See the
[Stakeholder Interview Playbook](/bmtechllc/portal/playbooks/discovery/stakeholder-interviews/).

## Exit criteria

Kickoff is complete when all four are true:

- Access is granted and verified, not merely promised
- All three stakeholder interviews are on calendars
- The client has confirmed the problem statement in writing
- The engagement folder exists with the kickoff notes committed
