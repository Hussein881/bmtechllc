Acme Corp Remote Work & Equipment Policy (2026):
1. Work Location: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
2. Hours: Core working hours for all remote team members are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST.
3. Equipment Stipend: An annual stipend of $500 is provided upon hiring anniversary.
4. Travel & Expense Reimbursement:
   Eligible equipment includes a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and headset purchased
   for approved work use. Travel meals are reimbursable up to $75 per day during
   approved business travel with itemized receipts; alcohol is not reimbursable.
   Submit expense reports within 10 business days of returning.
