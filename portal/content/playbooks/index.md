# Playbooks

## What belongs here

Guidance for phases of work that **vary by situation** and require judgment
to execute well. A playbook teaches how to think about a phase, not a fixed
script to follow line by line.

- How to run discovery, scoping, stakeholder interviews
- What to watch for, what "good" looks like, common failure modes
- Decisions the person doing the work still has to make themselves

If you catch yourself writing "it depends" while drafting a page, that's a
strong signal it belongs here rather than in Runbooks.

## What doesn't belong here

- A fixed procedure that should go the same way every time → **Runbooks**
- The stable "why" behind our overall approach → **Methodology**
- A copy-from-here artifact (a template file, a document skeleton) →
  **Templates**
- What actually happened when a playbook was used on a real engagement →
  **Engagements**

Playbooks can and should get updated as we learn — they're a living record
of how we currently think a phase should be run, not a historical account.
