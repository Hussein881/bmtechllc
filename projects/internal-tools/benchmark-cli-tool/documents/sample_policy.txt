ACME ENTERPRISE REMOTE WORK, EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT, AND TRAVEL POLICY
Document Reference: POL-2026-V4
Effective Date: January 15, 2026
Applicability: Global Operations & Full-Time Remote Staff

1. OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE
Acme Enterprise is committed to providing a flexible, modern workplace while maintaining operational excellence, fiscal responsibility, and internal alignment across time zones. This document defines the operational standards, schedule requirements, financial allowances, and travel guidelines governing all eligible staff members.

2. REMOTE WORK SCHEDULING AND ELIGIBILITY
Remote work is a privilege designed to enhance employee productivity and work-life balance while ensuring business continuity.

2.1 Remote Work Allocation
Full-time active employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week upon receiving prior written authorization from their direct department manager. Remote work allocations must be formally recorded in the HR portal prior to the start of each fiscal quarter. Temporary adjustments or remote work arrangements beyond 3 days per week require additional approval from the Vice President of Human Resources.

2.2 Core Working Hours
To maintain cross-functional collaboration, core working hours for all remote team members are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST. During core hours, remote personnel are expected to be online, reachable via primary team messaging channels, and available for scheduled virtual meetings. Outside of core hours, staff are expected to complete their remaining daily operational commitments according to their agreed-upon local working schedule.

3. HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT STIPEND AND REIMBURSEMENT
Acme Enterprise provides financial support to establish an safe, productive home office environment.

3.1 Annual Stipend Limit
The company provides an annual equipment stipend of $500 per calendar year for home office setup and ergonomics. Unused portions of the stipend do not rollover to subsequent calendar years and cannot be redeemed for cash value.

3.2 Eligible Reimbursement Items
Stipend funds must strictly be applied toward hardware and furniture designed for working comfort and productivity. Eligible items for reimbursement include monitor mounts, ergonomic chairs, standing desks, and mechanical keyboards. Hardware items such as personal gaming peripheral devices, television sets, external speakers, audio receivers, and general office decorations are explicitly ineligible for reimbursement.

3.3 Expense Submission Deadlines and Documentation
All reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of purchase with an itemized receipt uploaded directly to the finance portal. Submissions missing itemized breakdown receipts or filed after the 30-day window will be systematically rejected without appeal.

4. BUSINESS TRAVEL AND MEAL EXPENSES
This section dictates the mandatory corporate rules for official business travel and associated dining expenses.

4.1 Daily Meal Caps
During approved corporate business travel, domestic meal expenditures are capped at $75 per day. This daily allowance covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidental food purchases. International meal allowances follow specific regional tables maintained by corporate finance.

4.2 Receipt Documentation Requirements
Itemized receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips that do not detail the individual items purchased are not valid documentation under corporate audit rules. Alcohol purchases are non-reimbursable unless expressly approved in advance for client entertainment events.
