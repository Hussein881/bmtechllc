# Engagements

## What belongs here

A record of **what actually happened**, including the parts that didn't go
to plan. Engagement pages are grounded in one specific client and project —
they're the raw material that playbooks and methodology eventually get
refined from.

- Working notes taken during a real engagement
- Retros: what worked, what didn't, what we'd do differently
- Client-specific context that would be useful if this client comes back,
  or if someone else picks up related work later

Honesty matters more than polish here. A retro that only lists what went
well isn't useful to the next person who reads it.

## What doesn't belong here

- General guidance meant to apply beyond this one engagement → extract it
  into **Playbooks** or **Methodology** instead, and link back here as the
  example that prompted it
- A settled decision worth applying elsewhere → **Decisions**
- Rate cards, client credentials, anything sensitive that shouldn't sit next
  to internal retro notes — check visibility before writing

If a lesson here turns out to generalize, that's a sign it's time to write
a playbook — not a reason to rewrite this page.
