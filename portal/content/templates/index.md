# Templates

## What belongs here

Something you **copy and fill in**, not something you read. A template is a
skeleton for a real artifact — a document, a summary, a checklist you'll
hand to a client or use internally — with placeholders for the specifics.

- Discovery summary skeletons, report structures, checklist formats
- Anything whose value is in its shape, not its prose

## What doesn't belong here

- Guidance on how to fill the template out well, if it needs judgment →
  that guidance belongs in **Playbooks**, with a link to the template here
- A fixed procedure with no artifact to produce → **Runbooks**
- A one-off example of a filled-in artifact from a real engagement →
  **Engagements**

A template page should be mostly the artifact itself. If you find yourself
writing paragraphs explaining how to use it, split that explanation into a
playbook and link the two together.
