# Services

BenchmarkTech helps mid-market organizations move from AI curiosity to AI capability. We work across three service lines that map to where you are in your journey.

## AI Readiness Assessment

A structured four-week engagement that answers one question: *Is your organization ready to deploy AI, and if not, what needs to change first?*

We audit your data infrastructure, interview stakeholders, map your current workflows, and deliver a prioritized gap analysis with a 90-day action plan. You get a clear-eyed view of what AI can do for your organization today — and what groundwork is required before you can act.

**Who this is for:** Organizations that have seen AI in adjacent companies and want an honest evaluation of their own position before committing to a build.

## AI System Design & Build

For organizations that have cleared readiness, we design and build production-grade AI systems — primarily retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, workflow automation, and decision-support tools.

We do not prototype and disappear. Every engagement ends with a system your team owns: documented, tested, monitored, and supported through go-live.

**Who this is for:** Technical and product teams that need an external partner to scope, architect, and deliver a production AI system.

## Embedded AI Advisory

Ongoing strategic and technical advisory for organizations building internal AI capability. We work alongside your team on a retainer basis — reviewing architecture decisions, unblocking build teams, and helping leadership translate AI developments into coherent strategy.

**Who this is for:** Companies that have started building and need a senior technical voice without the overhead of a full-time hire.
