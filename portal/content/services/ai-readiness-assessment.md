# AI Readiness Assessment

A four-week structured engagement that gives you a clear, honest answer to one question: *Is your organization ready to deploy AI — and if not, what specifically needs to change first?*

## What we assess

### Data infrastructure

AI systems fail most often at the data layer, not the model layer. We assess:

- Availability and quality of the data sources your intended use case requires
- Whether data is accessible to an AI system (APIs, pipelines, permissions)
- Current data governance posture and any compliance constraints
- Gaps between the data you have and the data the use case demands

### Workflow and process fit

An AI system that improves a broken process produces better-broken outcomes. We map:

- The current workflow AI would touch, end to end
- Handoff points between humans and potential AI components
- Failure modes and what happens when the AI is wrong
- The measurement baseline you will use to evaluate improvement

### Stakeholder alignment

The most common reason AI projects stall is not technical — it is misaligned expectations across the people who own the outcome. We interview:

- The executive sponsor (what success looks like, risk tolerance, timeline pressure)
- The operational owner (day-to-day realities, workarounds, what actually takes time)
- The technical lead (infrastructure constraints, security concerns, integration requirements)

### Organizational capability

We assess whether you have the internal capability to own, maintain, and iterate on an AI system after delivery, or whether a capability-building phase is required alongside the build.

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | Format | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Gap Analysis** | Written report | Findings across the four assessment areas with severity ratings |
| **90-Day Action Plan** | Prioritized list | Sequenced steps to close the gaps that matter most |
| **Use Case Feasibility Matrix** | Table | 3–5 candidate use cases scored on impact, feasibility, and time-to-value |
| **Readout Session** | 60-min call | Live walkthrough with your team; questions answered |

## What happens after

One of three outcomes:

1. **Ready to build.** We propose a follow-on System Design & Build engagement scoped to the highest-value use case.
2. **Ready with prerequisites.** We hand you the action plan; you execute the prerequisites; we reconnect.
3. **Not the right time.** We tell you honestly, explain why, and what to watch for. We do not manufacture a build engagement from an assessment that doesn't support one.
