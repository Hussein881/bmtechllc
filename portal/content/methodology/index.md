# Methodology

## What belongs here

The **model**, not the execution. Methodology pages explain the shape of how
we deliver work and the reasoning behind it — the kind of thing that stays
true across many engagements and rarely changes month to month.

- The engagement model: phases, what each one is for, how they connect
- Principles that inform decisions across multiple engagements
- The "why" behind a way of working, not just the "how"

A useful test: if you rewrote this page for a completely different client
next month, would more than a few words change? If yes, it's not
methodology — it's probably a playbook or an engagement note.

## What doesn't belong here

- Step-by-step execution guidance for a phase → **Playbooks**
- Fixed, repeatable procedures → **Runbooks**
- A specific decision and its reasoning, scoped to one question →
  **Decisions**
- What actually happened on a specific engagement → **Engagements**

Methodology is the stable backdrop everything else happens against — it
should be the least frequently edited section in the KB.
