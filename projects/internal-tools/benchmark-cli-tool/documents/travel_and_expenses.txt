ACME ENTERPRISE GLOBAL TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY GUIDELINES
Document Reference: FIN-EXP-2026-03
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Applicability: All Operations, Sales, Executive Staff, and Traveling Consultants

1. CORPORATE TRAVEL REGULATION OVERVIEW
Traveling on behalf of Acme Enterprise requires balancing professional performance with fiscal discipline. All domestic and international business trips must be pre-approved by a department manager prior to booking flights, lodging, or transportation.

2. AIR TRAVEL AND LODGING ACCOMMODATION
Travelers must utilize the corporate booking engine for all airfare, rail, and hotel reservations to ensure negotiated corporate rates and automatic itinerary tracking.

2.1 Airfare Booking Guidelines
All international and domestic flights must be booked in Economy Class at least 14 days in advance of departure. Premium Economy or Business Class upgrades are prohibited unless transatlantic/transpacific flight duration exceeds 10 continuous hours, or when approved by an Executive Vice President.

2.2 Hotel Lodging Limits
Standard lodging reimbursement is capped at $250 per night in major metropolitan areas (including tax and local fees). In secondary markets, lodging is capped at $175 per night. Expenses exceeding these standard caps due to high-occupancy events or citywide conferences require advance written override approval from the Corporate Travel Desk.

3. EXPENSE SUBMISSION AND RECEIPT VERIFICATION
Accurate logging and timely reconciliation of business expenses are required for prompt employee reimbursement.

3.1 Portal Submission Window
All expense reports must be submitted into the finance portal within 30 days of trip completion. Reports submitted beyond this 30-day grace period will require Vice President sign-off and may be treated as taxable income or denied.

3.2 Receipt Requirements
Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Receipts must display the merchant name, date, itemized list of goods or services provided, total payment, and payment method used.
