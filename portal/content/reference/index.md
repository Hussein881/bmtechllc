# Reference

## What belongs here

Material you **look something up in**, not material you read top to bottom.
Reference pages answer "what does this mean?" or "what's the standard
value?" — they're consulted, not followed.

- Glossary entries and term definitions
- Standards, rate cards, checklists you consult mid-task
- Background/explainer material that supports other pages without itself
  being a procedure or a piece of guidance

## What doesn't belong here

- "How do I do X" guidance, even if it's informational → **Playbooks** or
  **Runbooks**, depending on whether judgment is required
- A fixed procedure → **Runbooks**
- Anything that only makes sense read in order, start to finish →
  **Onboarding**

If a page would be equally useful opened at a random heading with no
context, it's reference. If it needs to be read in sequence to make sense,
it belongs somewhere else.
