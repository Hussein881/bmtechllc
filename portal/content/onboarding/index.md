# Onboarding

## What belongs here

A **sequence**, not a pile. Pages here exist to be read in order by someone
new, to get them from "just joined" to "can work independently." Each page
should say what to read next.

- First-week orientation: tools, accounts, conventions
- The minimum context needed before touching client work
- Pointers into the rest of the KB, in the order they become relevant

## What doesn't belong here

- Standing reference material someone looks up occasionally → **Reference**
- How to execute a specific phase of engagement work → **Playbooks**
- Anything that isn't part of a first-read path — if it's not something every
  new hire reads early on, it probably lives elsewhere and gets linked to
  from here instead.

If a page here is more than a few weeks old and nobody has needed to update
it, that's a good sign — onboarding content should be the most stable
writing in the KB, because it's answering "how do things generally work,"
not "how do we do this specific thing right now."
