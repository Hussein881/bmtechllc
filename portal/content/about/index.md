# About BenchmarkTech

BenchmarkTech is an AI consulting firm that works with mid-market organizations on the hard part of AI: not the demos, but the deployment.

## What we do

We help organizations that have decided AI is relevant to their business figure out what to actually build, whether they're ready to build it, and how to build it so it keeps working after we leave.

Most of our work falls into one of three categories:

- **Readiness assessments** — structured evaluations of whether your organization can successfully deploy AI today, and what needs to change if not
- **RAG and retrieval system builds** — design and delivery of production document Q&A, knowledge retrieval, and decision-support systems
- **Advisory** — embedded strategic and technical guidance for teams that are building internal AI capability

## How we work

We do not hand you a slide deck and disappear. Every engagement has defined deliverables, defined acceptance criteria, and a handoff that leaves your team able to operate the system without us.

We also tell you when we are not the right choice for your problem — before you sign anything.

## Who we work with

Our clients are typically:

- Mid-market companies (50–5,000 employees) with technical leadership and a real business problem
- Teams that have tried to build AI capability internally and hit a wall
- Organizations with sufficient data and a specific use case, not teams still looking for a problem to solve

We are a specialist firm. We are not a general-purpose IT services provider or a large SI. That means faster engagement, tighter scoping, and a partner who does this specific work full-time.
