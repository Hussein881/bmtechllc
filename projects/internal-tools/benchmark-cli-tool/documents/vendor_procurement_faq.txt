ACME ENTERPRISE VENDOR ONBOARDING AND PROCUREMENT GUIDELINES
Document Reference: PROC-FAQ-2026-04
Effective Date: April 12, 2026
Target Audience: Project Managers, Finance Lead, Department Heads, and External Vendors

1. PROCUREMENT PROCESS OVERVIEW
Acme Enterprise enforces rigorous procurement standards to ensure risk mitigation, budget alignment, and competitive commercial terms for all third-party vendor relationships.

2. CONTRACT APPROVAL THRESHOLDS AND LEGAL REVIEW
Engagement contracts, statements of work (SOW), master services agreements (MSA), and software subscriptions must adhere to explicit financial threshold rules.

2.1 Threshold Approvals
Any vendor contract valued over $10,000 requires legal and procurement sign-off prior to execution. Contracts below $10,000 may be approved directly by a Department Director provided the expenditure is accounted for within approved department budgets.

2.2 Standard Payment Terms
Standard payment terms for all corporate vendors are Net 45 days from receipt of invoice following satisfactory verification of deliverables. Early payment terms (e.g., Net 15) must offer a minimum 2% early settlement discount and receive explicit written authorization from Corporate Treasury.
