Welcome. This page is the reading order for your first week. Work through it in
sequence — each section assumes the one before it.

> [!NOTE]
> Draft. This path reflects what we think matters most, but it has not yet been
> walked by someone genuinely new. Tell us where it fails.

## Day 1 — How the firm works

Read in this order:

1. [Engagement Model](/bmtechllc/portal/methodology/engagement-model/) — how a
   project runs from first conversation to handoff. This is the spine everything
   else hangs off.
2. [Glossary](/bmtechllc/portal/reference/glossary/) — skim it. You do not need
   to memorize it, but you should know it exists and that we use these terms
   precisely in client documents.

You should be able to explain, in your own words, what the four engagement phases
are and what makes each one complete.

## Day 2 — How we run discovery

Discovery is where most engagements are won or lost.

1. [Discovery Readiness Checklist](/bmtechllc/portal/playbooks/discovery/readiness-checklist/)
   — what must be true before discovery starts
2. [Stakeholder Interview Playbook](/bmtechllc/portal/playbooks/discovery/stakeholder-interviews/)
   — the highest-leverage activity we do

## Day 3 — The technical work

1. [Evaluating RAG Quality](/bmtechllc/portal/reference/evaluating-rag-quality/)
   — how we think about whether a system is actually good

## Day 4 — How we use this knowledge base

Read the [Guide](/bmtechllc/portal/guide/), particularly
[Writing Well](/bmtechllc/portal/guide/writing-well/).

Then do the exercise: **find something wrong or missing and fix it.** A typo, a
stale link, a page that assumes context you did not have. Open a PR.

This is the fastest way to learn the contribution loop, and a genuinely useful
first contribution — you are the only person who will ever read this KB without
prior context, and that perspective expires within about two weeks.

## Day 5 — Shadow real work

Sit in on whatever is live. Ask why, not what.

## After week one

You should be able to:

- Explain our engagement model to someone outside the firm
- Run a stakeholder interview with a senior person in the room
- Find any answer in this KB in under a minute, or know it is not here
- Add a page without asking how
