ACME ENTERPRISE CORPORATE EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Document Reference: HR-HB-2026-01
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Distribution: Company-Wide

1. WELCOME AND CODE OF CONDUCT
Welcome to Acme Enterprise. Our organizational culture is built upon mutual trust, accountability, integrity, and compliance with ethical business practices.

1.1 Professional Standards
Employees are expected to maintain professional standards during all internal and client interactions. Harassment, discrimination, verbal hostility, and unauthorized data disclosure are strictly prohibited and constitute grounds for immediate termination without prior warning.

1.2 Representation and Communications
Only designated media relations officers may speak publicly on behalf of Acme Enterprise. Employees must refrain from publishing proprietary information, strategic roadmaps, or non-public financial performance metrics on public social media channels.

2. TIME OFF AND LEAVE POLICIES
Acme Enterprise provides comprehensive time-off programs designed to support employee well-being and personal responsibilities.

2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues incrementally on a semi-monthly payroll schedule. Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days may carry over into the following calendar year; unused balance beyond 5 days is forfeited at midnight on December 31st unless governed otherwise by local statutory laws.

2.2 Request Procedure
PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted two weeks in advance through the employee self-service hub. Department managers are responsible for managing team schedules to ensure proper coverage during peak holiday periods.
