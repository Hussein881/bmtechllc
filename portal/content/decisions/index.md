# Decisions

## What belongs here

A record of **one settled question** and the reasoning behind it — written
so that six months from now, nobody has to re-derive why we chose this over
the alternatives.

- The question that was actually in dispute
- What we chose, and — just as important — what we didn't choose, and why
- Enough context that someone can tell whether the reasoning still holds

Numbered, one decision per page (see `0001-internal-only-knowledge-base.md`
for the format). Decisions are rarely edited after the fact — if
circumstances change enough to revisit one, write a new decision that
supersedes it rather than rewriting history.

## What doesn't belong here

- The stable "how we generally work" backdrop → **Methodology**
- A procedure that follows from a decision → **Playbooks** or **Runbooks**
- What happened when a decision played out on a real engagement →
  **Engagements**

If you're explaining "how," you're probably in the wrong section. Decisions
explain "why," once, and then get out of the way.
